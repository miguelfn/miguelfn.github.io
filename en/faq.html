About Chemical Suite

What is Chemical Suite?

Chemical Suite is a free Android app that bundles an interactive periodic table, a molecule viewer, an equation balancer, and calculators for stoichiometry, pH, molar mass, concentration/dilution, ionic character, gas laws and unit conversion, plus a scientific constants table and nomenclature tutorials with tests. It's built for students, teachers, researchers and chemistry professionals.

Is Chemical Suite free?

Yes, Chemical Suite is completely free. All of its tools and calculators are available at no cost.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Yes. Chemical Suite works 100% offline: the periodic table, all calculators, the molecule viewer, and the tutorials are available offline at any time.

Which devices are supported?

Chemical Suite is available for Android devices through Google Play. There is currently no iOS/iPhone version.

Do I need to create an account to use it?

No. The app is installed from Google Play and used directly, with no sign-up or user account required.

Can I remove the ads?

The free version of Chemical Suite includes every feature with no restrictions, but shows ads to help keep the app running. If you'd rather not see them, there's an optional paid, ad-free version that removes ads and supports the project's development.

Tools and content

What tools and calculators does the app include?

It includes a periodic table, an atomic and molecular weight calculator, a molecule viewer, equation balancing and stoichiometry, a pH calculator with acid dissociation constants, ionic character of a bond, solution concentration and dilution, gas law calculations, a unit converter, a scientific constants table, and inorganic and organic nomenclature tutorials and tests.

How many elements and data points does the periodic table include?

The periodic table covers all 118 known elements, with 40+ properties per element and data for 300+ stable and radioactive isotopes, plus trend charts across elements.

Is it useful for studying for chemical nomenclature exams?

Yes. Chemical Suite includes step-by-step inorganic and organic nomenclature tutorials along with 500+ test questions, designed for practice and exam preparation.

Where does the element and constants data come from?

Element property, isotope, and scientific constants data are based on standard scientific reference sources and are reviewed with each app update.

How can I report a bug or suggest an improvement?

You can email miguelfn@gmail.com directly to report bugs, ask questions, or suggest improvements for Chemical Suite.
