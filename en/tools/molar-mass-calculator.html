Atomic and molecular weight calculator

Enter any chemical formula — from a single element to a complex organic compound — and instantly get its exact molar mass in g/mol, along with the atomic breakdown of every element involved.

Get it on Google Play Back to tools

What it calculates

The weight calculator takes a chemical formula as text and calculates its molar mass by summing the atomic mass of each element times its subscript, correctly handling parentheses and hydrates.

Atomic mass

Weight of a single element, taken directly from the built-in periodic table.

Molecular mass

Weighted sum of every atom in the formula, with support for parentheses, subscripts, and hydrates (·nH2O).

Per-element breakdown

Shows what percentage of the total mass each element in the formula contributes.

How it works in the app

  • Open the weight calculator from the tools menu.
  • Type the chemical formula, respecting case and subscripts.
  • Instantly get the total molar mass in g/mol and the per-element breakdown.
  • Use the result directly in the molarity and concentration calculator.
