Chemical Fusion: discover compounds by combining elements

Add elements to the flask in the exact amounts a real compound needs — not one more, not one less — and watch it react instantly. 140+ compounds organized into 8 levels, from the simplest molecules to the most complex salts, with hints for when you're stuck and an educational card for every discovery.

Get it on Google Play Back to tools

What it includes

Chemical Fusion turns formula-writing into a discovery game: no memorizing rules in the abstract — every compound is "made" by combining exactly the atoms its real formula calls for, and pulling it off unlocks a card with its name, formula, and a fun fact about what it's actually used for.

Exact amounts

2 Fe + 3 O form Fe₂O₃, but just 1 Fe + 1 O form FeO: the ratio matters, just like in real chemistry.

8 progression levels

Simple molecules, oxides, halides, sulfides, hydrides & alloys, hydroxides & acids, complex salts, and a final challenge.

140+ compounds

From water and ammonia to sulfuric acid and copper sulfate, each with its formula, name, and real-world use.

Hint system

Stuck? Ask for a hint: it suggests the ingredients for the next compound within reach, without giving away the result.

Saved progress

Your collection of discoveries and the level you've reached are saved automatically between sessions.

How it works in the app

  • Tap elements from your palette to add them to the flask.
  • As soon as the mix exactly matches a real compound, it's discovered instantly.
  • Check your collection of discoveries or ask for a hint if you get stuck.
  • Clear each level to unlock new elements and more advanced compounds.
