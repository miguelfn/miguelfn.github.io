Equation balancing and stoichiometry calculations

Balance any chemical equation by entering reactants and products, then feed the result straight into the stoichiometry calculator to get moles, mass, or number of particles — with limiting reagent support.

Get it on Google Play Back to tools

What it includes

This tool combines two steps common to almost every chemistry problem: first it balances the equation so it satisfies the law of conservation of mass, then it lets you calculate amounts of reactants and products from the already-balanced coefficients.

Automatic balancer

Enter reactants and products without coefficients; the app finds the smallest integer coefficients that balance the equation.

Mole–mass–particle conversion

Convert between moles, grams, and number of particles (atoms or molecules) using the molar mass of each species.

Limiting reagent

Enter the starting amount of each reactant and the app identifies which one runs out first and how much product forms.

Reaction yield

Calculate the theoretical amount of product from the available reactants.

N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃ (balanced: 1 mol N₂ reacts with 3 mol H₂ to give 2 mol NH₃)

How it works in the app

  • Open the equation balancer and enter unbalanced reactants and products.
  • The app calculates the stoichiometric coefficients automatically.
  • Move to the stoichiometry calculator with the balanced equation.
  • Enter the known amount of one species (moles, grams, or particles) to solve for the rest.
