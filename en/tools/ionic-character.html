Ionic character of a bond calculator

Pick two elements and get the electronegativity difference between them, the estimated percentage of ionic character of the bond, and whether it is predominantly covalent, polar covalent, or ionic.

Get it on Google Play Back to tools

What it calculates

No bond is 100% ionic or 100% covalent: its real character depends on the electronegativity difference between the atoms involved. This tool takes the electronegativity values from the built-in periodic table and estimates that percentage automatically.

Electronegativity difference (Δχ)

Automatically calculated from the Pauling electronegativity values of both elements.

Percentage of ionic character

Estimates the ionic character of the bond based on Δχ, useful for predicting the type of solid formed.

Bond classification

Indicates whether the bond is nonpolar covalent, polar covalent, or ionic based on standard Δχ ranges.

Built-in electronegativity data

No need to look up values separately — they come directly from the app's periodic table.

Δχ = |χA − χB| → the larger Δχ, the greater the ionic character of the bond

How it works in the app

  • Open the ionic character calculator from the tools menu.
  • Select the two elements forming the bond.
  • The app automatically calculates Δχ and the percentage of ionic character.
  • Check the resulting bond classification (covalent, polar, or ionic).
