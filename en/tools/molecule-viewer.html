Molecule description and viewer

Explore 120+ inorganic and organic molecules in a 3D viewer with zoom and rotation: see their elements, geometry, and bonds. Each molecule includes its generic and IUPAC name, formula, weight, geometry type, description, and uses.

Get it on Google Play Back to tools

What it includes

The molecule viewer combines an interactive 3D model — with zoom and rotation to examine elements, geometry, and bonds — with a data card for each of the 120+ inorganic and organic molecules available.

3D viewer with zoom and rotation

Rotate and zoom the 3D model to examine each molecule from any angle.

Elements, geometry, and bonds

The viewer shows the elements, molecular geometry, and bonds of each compound.

120+ molecules

Inorganic and organic molecules.

Generic and IUPAC name

Look up the common name and the IUPAC name of each molecule.

Formula and weight

Chemical formula and weight of each molecule.

Geometry type

Molecular geometry of each compound.

Description and uses

Description of the molecule and its uses.

How it works in the app

  • Open the molecule viewer from the main menu.
  • Select the molecule you want to look up from the 120+ available.
  • Rotate and zoom the 3D model to examine its elements, geometry, and bonds.
  • Check the data card: generic and IUPAC name, formula, weight, geometry type, description, and uses.
